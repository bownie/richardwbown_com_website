In the recent [MapCamp](https://www.mapcamp.co.uk/), I had the pleasure of hearing a talk by [Susanne Kaiser](https://www.susannekaiser.net/#) about the ‘sweet spot’ of modern thinking when it comes to software business development, aligning business needs with customer needs and building software and teams that are optimised for both.

Her presentation covers the impressive sweep of three exciting parts of business strategy, design and organisational evolution namely [Wardley Mapping](https://learnwardleymapping.com/), [Domain Driven Design](https://www.bol.com/nl/nl/p/domain-driven-design/1001004001984629/) and [Team Topologies](https://www.bol.com/nl/nl/p/team-topologies/9200000104858741/).  Or as Susanne says in one of her talks – “the presentation with the most buzzwords in it”!

Pulling these three large topics together in a single 45-minute presentation is no mean feat and Susanne does a great job, although I’ve found it bears repeated readings and listenings. The slides for the presentation [can be found here](https://www.slideshare.net/SusanneKaiser3/architecture-for-flow-w-wardley-mapping-domaindriven-design-and-team-topologies) and you can download theme [here](https://www.susannekaiser.net/conferences/slides/QCon-London-2022.pdf) and there’s a great recording of her talk linked below.

[Watch Susanne Kaiser’s talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_U0VqmkpKqg)

## A Combined Approach

Any of these three subjects by themselves requires serious attention. So to combine three of them in a single presentation (and a coming book) is definitely ambitious. For us who need to be practical, it’s enough to know that this is a pattern which can exist. I think that it’s exciting to see this being brought together and look forward to finding out more about it in the future.
